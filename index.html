Why small-space picks are different

A "best desk" list written for suburban home offices is useless when your entire apartment is 400 square feet. A desk that needs a 6-foot wall, a sofa that only fits through a freight elevator, a storage unit deeper than your closet — none of it survives contact with a real studio.

That's why every guide on Small Space Savvy starts from the constraint: the room is small, and that's not changing. We compare folded and unfolded dimensions, clearance needs, weight for upper-floor apartments, and whether the thing actually fits through a standard 30-inch doorway. If a product only works in a big room, it doesn't make our lists — no matter how popular it is.
