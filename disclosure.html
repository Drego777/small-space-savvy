Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated: October 2026

The short version

Small Space Savvy is reader-supported. When you buy through links on our site, we may earn a commission from the retailer at no extra cost to you.

How it works

Many of our buying guides link to products on retailer sites. Those links are affiliate links: if you click one and make a purchase, the retailer pays us a small percentage of the sale. You pay exactly the same price either way — the commission comes out of the retailer's margin, not your wallet.

Amazon Associates

Small Space Savvy participates in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com. As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases.

What this doesn't change

  • Our picks are not for sale. No brand can pay for a recommendation, a ranking, or a review. Ever.
  • Commissions don't pick winners. We research first and link second — a product makes a guide because it's right for small spaces, not because it pays more.
  • We're not the seller. Purchases happen on the retailer's site under their terms, prices, and return policies. We don't process payments or handle shipping.

Why we disclose this

Because it's the law (FTC guidelines require clear disclosure of material connections) and because it's the right thing to do. You'll also see a short disclosure box at the top of every page that contains affiliate links.
