About Small Space Savvy

Small Space Savvy exists for one reason: most buying guides are written for people with room to spare. We write for the rest of us — apartment dwellers, studio renters, dorm residents, and RVers who measure furniture in inches, not feet.

How we pick products

Every guide starts from the constraint that the room is small and staying small. For each product we research:

  • Real dimensions — open, folded, and clearance. If the manufacturer hides the folded size, that's a red flag.
  • Fit realities — will it clear a standard 30-inch doorway? Fit up a walk-up stairwell? Survive a fifth-floor apartment?
  • Small-space trade-offs — wobble, weight, install difficulty, and whether it needs drilling (a lease-breaker for many renters).
  • Owner feedback — we read hundreds of verified buyer reviews, weighting ones that mention apartments, studios, and small rooms.
  • Value, not just price — a cheap product you replace twice is the most expensive option.
